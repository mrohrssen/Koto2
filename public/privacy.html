Koto Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Koto stores your username, password hash, game progress, creature collection, learning progress, known words, review history, settings, and optional API credentials you choose to save.

If you submit a bug report, Koto may collect your note, screenshot, account identifier, device/browser details, game state, and recent server diagnostics needed to investigate the issue.

Third-Party AI And Learning Services

When AI features are enabled, Koto may send gameplay text, vocabulary context, dialogue requests, and related learning context to the AI provider you configure, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or OpenRouter. If you add a Bunpro token, Koto may use it to fetch and submit grammar review data to Bunpro.

Koto does not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

How We Use Data

We use your data to run the game, save progress, personalize Japanese learning content, generate dialogue, process reviews, sync PvP and leaderboard features, troubleshoot bugs, and keep the service secure.

Retention And Deletion

You can delete your account inside Koto from Settings. Deletion removes your login, saved progress, learning data, encrypted API keys, and bug reports associated with your account, unless we are legally required to retain specific records.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the Koto developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
