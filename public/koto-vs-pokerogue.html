1. The Run

PokeRogue structures runs as 200 waves in 10-wave boss cycles across a biome graph. Koto structures runs as 10 areas, each with a fixed 30-room sequence. Both use a quadratic enemy level formula with nearly identical math.

Run Structure

PokeRogue

200 waves in 10-wave cycles. Waves 2-9 are regular fights, wave X0 is a boss, then heal/shop before biome transition. Creates a “heartbeat” rhythm: 9 fights → boss → breather.

Koto

10 areas × 30 rooms = 300 rooms per run. Fixed room layout: indices 5/11/17/23 are NPC battles, index 29 is boss. Other 25 slots: 45% encounter, 45% friendlyNpc, 10% whackAMole. 5% maxHP healed per room entry.

PokeRogue progresses temporally through waves; Koto spatially through rooms. PokeRogue’s 10-wave cycle is well-proven pacing; Koto’s 30-room areas provide deterministic structure.

2. Battle

PokeRogue uses a deep 18-type system with a 13-layer damage formula, crits, accuracy, weather, terrain, and hazards. Koto uses a streamlined 5-element cycle with a simplified formula optimized for vocab-card combat.

Damage Formula

PokeRogue
base = ((2×level/5+2) × power × ATK/DEF) / 50 + 2 Multipliers (13 layers): × multiTarget (0.75) × weather (1.5 or 0.5) × critical (1.5) × random (0.85–1.00) × STAB (1.5, +0.5 Tera, max 2.25) × typeEffectiveness (0/0.5/1/2/4) × burn (0.5 physical) × screens (0.5/0.67) × miscellaneous...
Koto
base = floor(((2×level/5+2) × power × ATK/DEF) / 10 + 2) final = floor(base × typeMult × variance) Multipliers (~7 layers): × STAB (1.5) × element effectiveness (0.67/1.0/1.5) × attackBuff/Debuff (percentage-based) × defenseBuff (percentage-based) × itemElementEdge × shield reduction (max 90%) × variance (0.8–1.2)
Same base formula structure. PokeRogue divides by 50, Koto by 10. PokeRogue has 13 multiplier layers; Koto has ~7. Koto trades complexity for clarity.

Type System

PokeRogue — 18 Types

Complex rock-paper-scissors web. Dual-typed defenders multiply both. Immunities (Normal → Ghost, Ground → Electric, etc.). Multipliers: 0×, 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×.

Koto — 5 Elements (Cycle)
Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood Super effective: next in cycle (1.5×) Resisted: previous in cycle (0.67×) Neutral: all others (1.0×) No immunities. No double SE.

Stats & Stat Modification

PokeRogue

6 stats: HP, ATK, DEF, SPATK, SPDEF, SPD
IVs: 0-31 per stat (5 bits from personality)
Natures: ±10% to two stats
Stat stages: -6 to +6, formula: max(2, 2+stage) / max(2, 2-stage)

Koto

4 stats: HP, ATK, DEF, MP
No IVs or Natures — deterministic from rarity
Rarity multipliers: common 1.0× → legendary 1.4×
Buffs/debuffs: percentage-based stacking (min 0.1×)

stat = floor(baseStat × (1 + (level-1) × 0.1))

Koto Design Philosophy

Koto optimizes for learning clarity over competitive depth. No RNG beyond variance, all actions simultaneous, creatures and moves are Japanese vocabulary. The simplified system lets players focus on language learning rather than battle optimization.

3. Party Building

PokeRogue offers deep customization through IVs, natures, abilities, evolution, and fusion. Koto uses a simplified collection-based system where creature power is determined by rarity.

Starter Selection

PokeRogue

Pick 1-6 starters from unlocked roster. Per-species cost (1-9 points). Configure gender, shiny, IVs, nature, form, ability, moves, Tera type. Higher-rarity = higher cost.

Koto

Select from meta.creatureCollection. Rarity-based cost: common=3, uncommon=4, rare=6, epic=7, legendary=8. Max 10 team points. Default starters: hikaribon, hanatchi, tsukimochi.

5. Economy & Progression

PokeRogue has layered engagement loops: exponential money, tiered vouchers, 130+ achievements, ribbons, and timed events. Koto uses transparent, deterministic progression: flat credits, progression tokens, and a small meta-upgrade shop.

In-Run Currency

PokeRogue — Money

Exponential growth per wave (~175 early, thousands late). Five money multipliers: MoneyMult (+20%/stack), Happy Hour (2×), Meowth (damage-based), Coin Case (10%/turn interest). Rewards planning and holding.

Koto — Credits

Flat 15 credits per kill. Starting: 55 credits. Additional sources: quiz rooms, whack-a-mole, creature dealer sales. No credit multipliers exist. Predictable but lacks scaling tension.

Meta-Progression

PokeRogue

Vouchers: 4 tiers (Regular/Plus/Premium/Golden) for gacha egg pulls. Earned from achievements and boss victories.
Achievements: 130+ with score-based tiers. Drive voucher economy.
Ribbons: 40+ per species, cosmetic prestige from challenge completions.
Timed Events: 10+ annual events with multipliers (shiny, friendship, etc.).

Koto

Progression Tokens: +1 per boss defeat, +1 per befriend. ~20/run.
Meta Shop: 3 upgrades × 5 levels (HP/ATK/XP Boost). Cost: 1,2,3,4,5 per level. 45 tokens to max all.
Achievements: 4 total (First Victory, Veteran Hunter, Dungeon Master, Perfect Run). No rewards yet.
No timed events, no ribbons.

6. Gacha & Eggs vs Befriending

This is the biggest philosophical split between the two games. PokeRogue gates roster access behind a gacha system with pity mechanics. Koto uses skill-based befriending through vocabulary quizzes.

Acquisition Model

PokeRogue — Gacha

3 machines: Move (egg move focus), Legendary (rare species), Shiny (2× shiny rate).
4 egg tiers: Common (10 waves, 79.7%), Rare (25 waves, 17.2%), Epic (50 waves, 2.7%), Legendary (100 waves, 0.4%).
Pity systems: Tier pity (9/59/412 pulls), species pity (9 dupes), batch guarantees (10-pull ≥1 Rare).
Cost: Vouchers from achievements/bosses.

Koto — Befriend Quiz

Trigger: 10% on killing blow (BEFRIEND_QUIZ_CHANCE = 0.10). 100% if player owns ≤1 creature.
Quiz: Shown creature name in Japanese; pick correct English name from 3 options.
Correct: Creature added to permanent collection.
Wrong: Creature counter-attacks (real damage).
Cost: Free — earned through gameplay knowledge.

Befriending directly serves the game’s educational purpose — knowing creature names in Japanese is both the skill test and the learning outcome. No paywall, no FOMO. PokeRogue trades this for collection excitement via rare tiers and shinies.

8. AI & Trainers

PokeRogue has sophisticated enemy AI with score-based move selection, gym leaders, and a fixed battle schedule. Koto uses simpler AI but adds NPC personality systems with combat skills and persistent bonds.

Enemy AI

PokeRogue — 3-Tier AI

RANDOM: Pick moves at random.
SMART_RANDOM: 62.5% best move, 37.5% next-best.
SMART: Score-based: user benefit + target benefit × type effectiveness × STAB. Diminishing probability down ranked list.

Target: Priority order: super-effective targets → neutral targets → weakest by HP%. All sorted by lowest HP%.

9. Vocabulary & Learning KOTO-UNIQUE

Koto’s entire reason for existing. A comprehensive Japanese vocabulary learning engine built on the i+1 comprehensible input principle, integrated with JPDB’s spaced repetition database, internal FSRS scheduling, and AI-powered text repair.

JPDB Integration

Server-side integration with jpdb.io for word state management. Rate-limited (500ms min interval) with circuit breaker (5-min cooldown). Supports 9 word states: new, learning, known, never-forget, due, failed, suspended, blacklisted, not-in-deck. Review grades 1-5 (nothing → easy).

i+1 Principle Enforcement

Every sentence shown to the player must contain at most 1 unknown word. Enforced through a 3-phase pipeline:

  • Parse: Split narration into sentences, morphological analysis via JPDB
  • Check: Count unknowns (excluding 156 allowed particles/grammar words)
  • Repair: If violations found, AI rewrites using known vocabulary. Max 2 retry attempts. Falls back to original if repair fails.

Vocabulary Manager

Per-user suggestion system with intelligent caching. Targets 12 word suggestions per narration with ratio: 60% due, 25% learning, 15% known. Cache expires every 30 minutes. Tracks last 50 words to avoid repetition. Priority scoring: due=1.0, failed=0.9, learning=0.7, known=0.3.

Learning Rooms

Room TypePurposeMechanics
Speed ReviewJPDB spaced repetition review5 initial cards + replacement pool. Grades submitted to JPDB. Targets 10 cards per session.
Word DiscoveryIntroduce new vocabulary2 new words per room. Sorted by JPDB frequency rank. Daily limit: 10 words (configurable). Resets at midnight Tokyo time (JST).

Hiragana SRS

71 hiragana characters across 15 rows. Uses FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) algorithm. Row-based unlock: row N unlocks when all cards in row N-1 have ≥1 review. Persisted per-user as JSON.

JLPT Grammar Constraints

All AI-generated text respects the player’s selected JLPT level (N5–N1). Grammar specifications injected into every AI prompt — from basic です/ます at N5 to literary/classical forms at N1.

10. NPC Dialogue System KOTO-UNIQUE

A full AI-driven dialogue engine with persistent NPC memory, SillyTavern-inspired character cards, vocabulary-constrained generation, and 3-round tone-based conversations that affect NPC bonds.

Architecture Overview

7-layer prompt assembly: system instructions → vocabulary constraints → character card → lorebook → NPC memory → anti-repetition → task prompt. Supports OpenAI, Anthropic Claude (with prompt caching), Google Gemini, and OpenRouter.

Character Cards

Each NPC has a personality card with: traits, quirk, example dialogue (3+ lines), state-conditional goals (possessed/glitching/liberated), optional lorebook keys, and a teaching word. Fallback cards auto-generated from creature data for entities without authored cards.

Dialogue Format

ComponentDescription
greetingNPC greets the player before interaction
defeatLineNPC’s line if player loses
freedLineNPC’s line when calmed/befriended
3 roundsEach: npcLine + 3 options (positive/neutral/negative tone)

Bond change: Each round’s tone choice adds +1/0/-1. After 3 rounds, total delta clamped to [-1, 0, +1] and applied to persistent bond score.

Vocabulary Repair Pipeline

After AI generates dialogue, all 15 text fields are checked against player’s known vocabulary via JPDB. If any field has >1 unknown word, a multi-turn repair loop sends the violations back to the AI with instructions to substitute simpler words. Max 3 repair attempts.

NPC Memory & Staleness

Per-NPC persistent state: encounter counters, flags (liberated/befriended/betrayed), rolling 5-entry encounter log, narrative arc text, bond score. Dialogue cache invalidates when vocab grows >3% or >10 words, or memory snapshot changes.

DM Narration

Event-driven Japanese narration for all game events (combat start, attacks, victory, defeat, exploration). Narration memory tracks recent events and used phrases to avoid repetition. Sensory rotation (sight/sound/touch/smell) varies descriptions. 4-6 sentence minimum with light-novel style.

11. Audio & TTS KOTO-UNIQUE

Koto features real-time Japanese text-to-speech via VOICEVOX with 200+ voice styles, personality-mapped NPC speakers, and a dual caching system. PokeRogue uses pre-recorded Pokemon cries — no narration TTS.

VOICEVOX Integration

2-step synthesis pipeline: audio query (text → phonetic parameters) → synthesis (parameters → WAV). Customizable per-call: speed (0.5-2.0), pitch, intonation, volume. Default narrator: speaker 13 (玄野武宏 Cool). Health check with 2s timeout.
